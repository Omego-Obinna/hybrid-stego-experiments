# Licensing status

This packaging step does not choose a new licence for the author's code,
manuscript, data or third-party material. No repository-wide open-source licence
was present in the source snapshot used for this release. Public availability
alone does not grant unrestricted reuse.

The original source notices and supplied third-party licence texts are retained
under `third_party/licenses/`. These apply to their respective components only.
The HSMNet snapshot and several component implementation snapshots did not
provide a separate licence file in the material retrieved; this package makes
no additional grant for them. See `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md` and the upstream authors
for their terms. Dataset, generator-weight and journal-template terms remain
separate.

An author-selected root licence can be added when the corresponding rights and
intended terms are established. It should not overwrite third-party notices.
