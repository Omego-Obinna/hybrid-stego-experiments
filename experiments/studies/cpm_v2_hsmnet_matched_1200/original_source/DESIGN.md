# Fixed native image comparison panel

Prepared before any production native case or held-out scoring.

1,200 distinct BOSSBase covers: 700 training, 200 validation, 300 test, all from the audited content-grouped partition. All covers used in the five earlier revision studies are excluded. Three application payloads: 0.10, 0.20 and 0.40 bpp.

3,600 new revised CPM attempts use unchanged accepted protocol, image worker and fused-0.75 embedder. A declared twelve-pair public bank supplies 65,536-byte prefixes. Fresh local fixture secrets and saved random messages are used. No failed attempt is replaced or retried. Exactly recovered images with pixel changes exceeding one level are retained and explicitly counted. Unexpected operational failures or incorrect accepted CPM plaintexts halt the pipeline.

14,400 baseline records are reused after native-image hash audits. The recorded classical implementations are S-UNIWARD, MiPOD, WOW and HILL. The local MiPOD variance estimator is an approximation, not a certified reference implementation. Historical recovery is source-reported; the missing original master-secret configuration prevents independent expected-byte reconstruction.

A separate sequential development panel uses 30 previous-pilot covers x three payloads x five methods: 450 fresh cases. Saved messages match within each cover/payload. This supplies independent known-byte recovery and fresh core/runtime observations. Core embedding and whole-process time are separated; actual serialized protocol bytes are reported for CPM. Single-carrier baseline primitive costs do not imply equivalent authentication/replay protection.

Detectors and paired fidelity use common-valid blocks symmetrically across all five methods; every attempted image remains in all-attempt accounting. Fixed minimum complete counts are 600/150/240 at each payload. Full SRM features feed method-specific FLD ensembles (three validation-only subspace candidates, two fixed seeds). HSMNet's official architecture is trained on an equal-method mixture for each payload and two seeds, with native 512-pixel inputs, 100 fixed epochs and validation-only checkpoint selection. This is a bounded training protocol, not a reproduction of published detector scores. Detailed settings are in run_config.json.

Test selections are locked before one scoring pass. All 36 selected checkpoint prediction sets are independently reproduced. Cover-cluster uncertainty preserves pairing across methods and payloads; twelve CPM-minus-baseline contrasts per detector have family-adjusted intervals. Data, model, selection, prediction, and all-attempt evidence stay local and preserved.

No input combines source covers, keys, seeds or verification outcomes with image-content classifier features. This panel measures empirical image detectability/fidelity/cost, not a cryptographic epsilon or full-view CP-UNL guarantee. Native generative and distributed-carrier experiments are separate panels with their own benign sources and formats.

Development smoke checks passed known-byte native-STC round trips for all four baseline implementations, full SRM dimensionality/finite values, FLD fit/prediction, and HSMNet native-resolution GPU forward/backward. Preparation attempts with no production trials remain archived as *_preparation_attempt*_no_trials.
